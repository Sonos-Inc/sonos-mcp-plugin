# Sonos

Control your Sonos system

Control your Sonos system directly from Cursor and Grok. Play music and radio
from your connected music services, or your Sonos favorites and Sonos playlists.
Group and ungroup rooms for synchronized multi-room listening, move the music
from one room to another, and see what's playing anywhere in your home. Set
volume and mute on a speaker or group.

All you need to enjoy Sonos, without leaving the conversation.

## Requirements

- A Sonos account with access to a compatible Sonos system and online players.
- Cursor and/or Grok with MCP support and permission to load this plugin.
- Sonos OAuth authorization for the connected account.
- For music service playback, a supported music service must be configured on Sonos.
  Service subscriptions and content availability may vary.

Sonos MCP currently supports US-English. See the [Sonos setup documentation](https://support.sonos.com/article/control-your-sonos-system-with-ai-using-sonos-27mcp)
for supported music services and current availability.

## Example Requests

- Discover the Sonos system and check current playback: "What Sonos rooms do I have, and what's playing in them?"
- Set the volume of a Sonos player: "Set the volume of my Sonos player to 20."
- Play music from a connected service: "Play BTS on Sonos."
- Play a Sonos Favorite for a routine: "I'm about to do Pilates. Can you put on one of my Sonos Favorites that fits the mood?"
- Group rooms and start a playlist: "Group all my Sonos rooms together and play my Pilates playlist. My family is about to start Pilates together."
